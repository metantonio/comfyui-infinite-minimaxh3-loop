GLOBAL:

Maintain exact character identities, facial features, hair, clothing, environment, lighting, and semi-realistic next-generation 3D visual style from the opening frame across all loops.

* Link: young child, blonde hair, blue eyes, pointed Hylian ears, wearing his iconic green tunic with leather details, barefoot.
* Link must remain visually identical whenever he appears.
* The opening environment is a rustic Kokiri-style dwelling with woven straw bedding, natural materials, dark fabric curtains, warm dim lighting, and cinematic shadows.
* The opening image is only the visual starting point of the film.
* The original poster composition must transition into a fully immersive cinematic world.
* Do NOT show Zelda yet.
* Do NOT show the Master Sword yet.
* Do NOT introduce other major characters yet.

LOGO REMOVAL:

The "THE LEGEND OF ZELDA — OCARINA OF TIME" logo, Master Sword, Hylian Shield, and all promotional poster graphics are only part of the reference image.

They must completely disappear during the opening camera transition.

The logo and poster graphics must NEVER reappear in any subsequent loop.

The final result must look like a cinematic fantasy movie, not a game poster.

NARRATOR:

Use a deep an calm, powerful, imposing male English narrator voice.

The narrator should sound like an ancient storyteller describing the beginning of a legendary prophecy.

Slow, deliberate delivery.

Strong dramatic pauses.

Authoritative, mysterious, and cinematic.

CAMERA STYLE:

Use cinematic camera movement throughout.

The camera may travel rapidly between locations when the narration describes the wider world.

Use aerial establishing shots, sweeping landscape movements, long cinematic transitions, and controlled dramatic camera movements.

When showing danger or evil, do not immediately reveal everything.

Use distance, silhouettes, shadows, atmospheric haze, backlighting, and partially obscured figures to create mystery.
